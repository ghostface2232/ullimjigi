# Models

These models are built in Blender. The scripts live in `ref/blender/` (see its README), except for
`mn_heart.glb`, whose generator and original texture inputs are in the Heart boss round’s evidence/work bundle.
The files below use third-party scans as their form source.

## Third-party sources

The scattered rocks (`pr_rocks.glb`, `pr_rocktex.glb`) and the rolling boulder (`pr_boulder.glb`,
`pr_bouldertex.glb`) are restyled from photogrammetry scans. Each rock keeps the old model's
layout of masses and bounding box. Large scan-derived planes carry the silhouette and smaller fracture
faces break up the stepped slabs. A gentle spatial warp bends their shared edges. Short worn stretches
and occasional shallow chips interrupt the sharper ridges. The decimated meshes use baked normals
from this sculpt and fine scan relief. Painted albedo separates pale tops, cool sides and dark recesses.
