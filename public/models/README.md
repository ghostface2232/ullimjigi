# Models

Every model here is built by the Blender scripts in `ref/blender/` (see its README). Most are made
from scratch; the files below use third-party scans as their form source.

## Third-party sources

The scattered rocks (`pr_rocks.glb`, `pr_rocktex.glb`) and the rolling boulder (`pr_boulder.glb`,
`pr_bouldertex.glb`) are restyled from photogrammetry scans: each scan is remeshed, flattened into
a few broad planes with stepped secondary blocks, decimated, and baked (normal map: the designed
planes plus the fine band of the scan). The albedo is painted, not taken from the scans.
